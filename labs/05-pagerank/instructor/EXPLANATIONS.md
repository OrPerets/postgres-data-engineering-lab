# Instructor guide — PageRank
Basic 3-page graph first iteration: A=1/3, B=1/6, C=1/2; second: A=1/2, B=1/6, C=1/3. Four-page graph with D dangling, d=0.85: A=0.303125 B=0.196875 C=0.409375 D=0.090625. Mass sums to 1. For each step, compute local contribution sum, dangling mass, then (1-d)/N+d*(incoming+dangling/N). Convergence uses L1 distance. Structure messages preserve adjacency lists across MapReduce iterations. With more dampening toward links, convergence may be slower and ranks less uniform. A hot target causes shuffle skew. Physical multiprocessing benchmark differs from genuine multi-node distributed computing.
